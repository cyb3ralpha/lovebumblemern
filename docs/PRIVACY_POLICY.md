# Privacy Policy

## Information Collection
We collect information necessary to provide and improve our services:
- Account information (name, email, age, photos)
- Profile information (bio, interests, location)
- Usage data and analytics
- Communication preferences

## Information Usage
Your information is used to:
- Provide and maintain our services
- Send notifications and updates
- Improve user experience
- Conduct analytics and research
- Comply with legal obligations

## Information Sharing
We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except:
- Service providers (Cloudinary for photos, Stripe for payments)
- Law enforcement when required by law
- With consent for specific purposes

## Data Retention
- Active user data is retained as long as the account is active
- Deleted account data is removed within 30 days
- Chat message data can be deleted by users
- Administrative logs are retained for 1 year

## User Rights
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request data deletion
- Export your data
- Opt-out of marketing communications

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@lovebumble.com
